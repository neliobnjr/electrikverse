electrikverse

electrikverse is a tool that creates short educational videos about electric vehicles (EVs) and uploads them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account (@electrikverse).

It uses TikTok Login Kit (scope user.info.basic) to identify the authorizing account and the TikTok Content Posting API (scope video.upload) to upload videos as drafts. Nothing is published automatically; the creator reviews and publishes each draft inside TikTok.
